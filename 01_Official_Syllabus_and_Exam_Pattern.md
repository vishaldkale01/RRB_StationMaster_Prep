# RRB Station Master (CEN 08/2026) — Complete Exam & Study Guide

---

## 1. Exam Blueprint & Key Rules

* **Duration**: **120 Minutes** (2 Hours)
* **Total Questions**: **100 Questions** (1 mark each)
* **Marking**: $+1$ for correct, **$-1/3$ penalty** for wrong answers.
* **Stage**: **Single Stage CBT** $\rightarrow$ Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) $\rightarrow$ DV & Medical.
* **Selection Merit**: **70% CBT Score + 30% CBAT Score**.
* **Medical Standard**: **A-2** (Distant vision 6/9, 6/9 without glasses. **LASIK/Eye surgery is strictly disqualified**).
* **Application Dates**: **27.10.2026 to 25.11.2026** on [www.rrbapply.gov.in](https://www.rrbapply.gov.in).

---

## 2. Official 100-Mark Syllabus (Zero General Knowledge!)

### Section 1: Basic Science, Mathematics & Data Analysis (60 Marks)
1. **Basic Science & Mathematics (30 Marks)**:
   * **Mathematics**: Number System, LCM & HCF, Ratio & Proportions, Averages, Percentages, Profit, Loss & Discounts, Time & Work (Power & Work), Speed, Time, Distance, Linear Equations, Algebra, Arithmetic Progression (AP), Geometry, Trigonometry, Area & Volumes (Mensuration), Basic Probability & Statistics.
   * **Basic Physics**: Units & Measurements, Mass, Weight & Density, Work, Power & Energy, Kinetic Energy, Friction, Speed & Velocity, Relative Velocity, Heat & Temperature, Basic Electricity, Levers & Simple Machines.
2. **Data Analysis & Interpretation (30 Marks)**:
   * Multi-source Data Analysis: Tabular data, Charts, Graphs, **Scatter Plots**, Pie Charts, Statistical Curve Distributions, Venn Diagrams, Data Sufficiency, and Data Arrangement.

### Section 2: Logical Capability (20 Marks)
* **Logical Reasoning**: Binary Logic, Syllogisms, Clocks & Calendars, Assumptions, Blood Relations, Family Tree, Logic-based Puzzles.
* **English Reading Comprehension**: Passages in English tested on: Main Idea, Supporting Idea, Application, Logical Structure & Style.

### Section 3: Mental Reasoning (20 Marks)
* Analogy (pair relationships)
* Series Completion (number sequences)
* Coding-Decoding
* Ranking & Seating Arrangements
