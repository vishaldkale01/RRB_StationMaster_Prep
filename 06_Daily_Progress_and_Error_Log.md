## 🛠️ The 5-Category Metacognitive Error Log

> Every time you get a question wrong in practice or mocks, log it here. **Do not just write the solution—classify the cognitive breakdown.**

### Error Categories:
1. **Concept Gap (CG)**: Didn't know the core formula or rule.
2. **Calculation Error (CE)**: Arithmetic slip, sign mistake, table recall error.
3. **Misread (MR)**: Misread the question stem, didn't notice units or "NOT".
4. **Time-Management Failure (TM)**: Stuck too long on one question, rushed at the end.
5. **Bad Guess (BG)**: Guessed without eliminating at least 2 options.
