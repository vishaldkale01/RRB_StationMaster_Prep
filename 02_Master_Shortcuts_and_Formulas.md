# Master Formulas & 30-Second Shortcuts Cheat Sheet

## 1. Speed Unit Conversion (The 18 & 5 Rule)
* **Rule**: Table of $18\text{ km/h}$ matches Table of $5\text{ m/s}$:
  * $18\text{ km/h} = 5\text{ m/s}$
  * $36\text{ km/h} = 10\text{ m/s}$
  * $54\text{ km/h} = 15\text{ m/s}$
  * $72\text{ km/h} = 20\text{ m/s}$
  * $90\text{ km/h} = 25\text{ m/s}$
* Multiply by $\frac{5}{18}$ to convert $\text{km/h} \rightarrow \text{m/s}$.
* Multiply by $\frac{18}{5}$ to convert $\text{m/s} \rightarrow \text{km/h}$.

## 2. Time & Work (The LCM / Laddoo Method)
* Total Work = $\text{LCM of days}$.
* Efficiency (Daily Work) = $\frac{\text{Total Work}}{\text{Individual Days}}$.
* Combined Time = $\frac{\text{Total Work}}{\text{Sum of Efficiencies}}$.
* *Example*: A in 10 days, B in 15 days $\rightarrow$ Total = 30 units. A = 3/day, B = 2/day $\rightarrow 30 / (3+2) = 6\text{ days}$.

---

## 3. Consecutive Compound Interest Rate Shortcut
* When amounts for two consecutive years are given:
  $$\text{Rate } (r) = \frac{\text{Amount}_{n+1} - \text{Amount}_n}{\text{Amount}_n} \times 100$$
* *Example*: ₹1,600 in Year 2 and ₹1,700 in Year 3:
  $$\text{Rate} = \frac{100}{1600} \times 100 = \mathbf{6.25\%}$$

## 4. Syllogisms Arrow Rule
* Draw arrows: "All A are B" ($A \rightarrow B$), "All B are C" ($B \rightarrow C$).
* Combined chain: $A \rightarrow B \rightarrow C$.
* **Forward is VALID**: All A are C $\checkmark$
* **Backward is INVALID**: All C are A $\times$

## 5. Non-Negotiable Squares (1 to 25)
```
 1² = 1       6² = 36      11² = 121     16² = 256     21² = 441
 2² = 4       7² = 49      12² = 144     17² = 289     22² = 484
 3² = 9       8² = 64      13² = 169     18² = 324     23² = 529
 4² = 16      9² = 81      14² = 196     19² = 361     24² = 576
 5² = 25     10² = 100     15² = 225     20² = 400     25² = 625
```
* **Numbers Ending in 5**: Multiply first digit by $(digit + 1)$ and append $25$:
  * $65^2 \rightarrow 6 \times 7 = 42 \rightarrow \mathbf{4225}$.
  * $35^2 \rightarrow 3 \times 4 = 12 \rightarrow \mathbf{1225}$.
